# Submission readiness

The manuscript in this repository is a research render, not an official NeurIPS-formatted submission.

For NeurIPS 2026, the conference requires the official 2026 LaTeX style, a single PDF containing the paper, references, optional appendices, and the mandatory paper checklist. The main text may use up to nine content pages. Anonymous submissions must also remove identifying information from linked or supplementary material.

Before any conference submission:

- compile the manuscript with the official style file supplied by the conference;
- use the anonymous author mode required for double-blind review;
- complete the official checklist in the required template;
- verify page limits after recompilation;
- anonymize code, metadata, artifact links, and supplementary material;
- rerun every machine-verifiable claim and archive the resulting outputs;
- keep external-benchmark and human-study claims absent until those experiments are actually executed.

The scientific content in this repository is intentionally kept separate from venue-specific formatting so that the research claims do not depend on a copied or modified third-party template.
